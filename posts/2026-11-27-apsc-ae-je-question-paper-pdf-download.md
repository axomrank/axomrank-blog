## Frequently asked questions

### Are the AE and JE PDFs grouped by department?

Yes. The exam headings distinguish Public Works, Public Health Engineering, Water Resources and other recruitments in this archive.

### How do I tell whether a PDF is the technical paper?

Use the subject in the download table and confirm it on the first page of the booklet. A General Studies and General English label describes a common paper.
