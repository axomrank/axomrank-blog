**In the DEE Assam cycle advertised on 8 January 2025, TET counted for 85% of Lower Primary merit and 80% of Upper Primary merit.** The Directorate of Elementary Education published this breakdown in its February 2026 provisional merit notice for advertisements E-573006/28 and E-573006/32. Use the example to understand that cycle and check your own notice for a later recruitment.

## LP and UP weights from the DEE notice

| Component | Lower Primary | Upper Primary |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Higher Secondary | 5% | 5% |
| TET | 85% | 80% |
| Professional qualification | 5% | 5% |
| Graduation | 0% | 5% |
| Extra-curricular activities (ECA) | 5% | 5% |
| **Total** | **100%** | **100%** |

The weights tell you the maximum contribution of each component. For a percentage-based component, multiply the percentage expressed as a fraction by its weight. Use the graduation marks specified for the post. The UP Assistant Teacher table, for example, uses the major-subject marks where the applicant had a major. Apply DEE's evidence rules separately to any ECA claim.

## Worked examples using hypothetical results

Suppose an applicant has **80% in Higher Secondary, 75% in TET, 80% in the applicable professional qualification and 70% in the applicable graduation marks**. Assume no ECA marks are credited.

- **LP:** Higher Secondary contributes 4, TET contributes 63.75 and the professional qualification contributes 4. The total is **71.75 out of 100**.
- **UP:** Higher Secondary contributes 4, TET contributes 60, the professional qualification contributes 4 and graduation contributes 3.5. The total is **71.50 out of 100**.

These examples explain the weighting, including why a candidate's TET percentage and weighted merit marks differ. Eligibility and the qualifying TET level still depend on the post. See the [Special TET paper guide]({{base}}/assam-special-tet-paper-1-vs-paper-2/) and [Assam TET exam hub]({{base}}/assam-tet/).

## Read cut-offs and merit lists for the exact post

DEE published district- and category-wise cut-offs for the cited cycle. Match the advertisement, post, district and category before comparing a mark. The provisional notice says its list was prepared using online and physically verified credentials and would be replaced once the next provisional or final list appeared. Follow the latest list and instructions issued by DEE.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does TET qualify me for an appointment by itself?

Passing TET can meet an eligibility condition. The appointment depends on the instructions, eligibility and selection process for the advertised teacher post.

### Did graduation contribute to LP merit in this cycle?

The cited notice gives graduation zero weight for LP merit and 5% for UP merit. Eligibility conditions are a separate part of the advertisement.

### Why can the same TET percentage yield a different LP and UP contribution?

The weights were different. At 75%, the TET component contributes 63.75 in the LP example and 60 in the UP example.
