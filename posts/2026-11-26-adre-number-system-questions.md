## Frequently asked questions

### Which number system topics appeared most in these ADRE papers?

Fractions were the largest tagged subtopic with 17 questions. LCM and HCF followed with 11, and square roots had nine.

### How do HCF and LCM help solve a two-number problem?

For positive integers, the HCF multiplied by the LCM equals the product of the integers. Use the other condition to identify the factor pair.

### How do I solve questions where every remainder is one below the divisor?

Add one to the unknown number. The result is divisible by each of the stated divisors, so use their LCM.
