## Frequently asked questions

### Who was the Mughal commander at Saraighat?

Ram Singh of Amber led the expedition sent by Aurangzeb.

### Who was the last Ahom ruler?

Purandar Singha is the expected answer in the cited ADRE and Assam Police questions.
