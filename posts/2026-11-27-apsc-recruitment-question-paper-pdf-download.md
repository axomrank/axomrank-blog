## Frequently asked questions

### How can I find a PDF for a particular APSC post?

Open the guide's contents and select the section named for the post, or search the page for its designation.

### Is the cycle year always the day the test was held?

For departmental APSC papers it is the advertisement year in the source catalog. Read the booklet cover for its printed examination date.
