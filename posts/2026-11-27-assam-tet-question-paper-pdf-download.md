### Which Assam TET folder contains the 2021 Higher Secondary booklets?

Look under Assam Higher Secondary TET. The archive offers Paper II Commerce and Option C. The available Option B source file is damaged.

### How can I recognise an incomplete Assam TET booklet?

A row marked partial scan indicates known missing pages. Check the page sequence and optional language section before treating any download as a complete test.
