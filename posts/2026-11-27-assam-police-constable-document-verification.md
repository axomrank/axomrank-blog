**For Assam Police Constable PET and PST, pack your admit card, an accepted photo ID, the originals of your uploaded certificates and one set of self-attested photocopies.** This checklist follows sections 6.2, 7.1 and 7.6 of SLPRB advertisement 727/2025/94. Read the instructions on your own admit card and check for later notices before travelling.

## What to put in your folder

| Document | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| PET and PST admit card and photo ID | Keep both together for identity verification. The advertisement gives Aadhaar, voter ID and driving licence as examples of identity proof. |
| Proof of age | Bring the HSLC or equivalent examination admit card or certificate used in your application. |
| Education records | Bring the pass certificate and marksheet for the qualification applicable to your post. Check whether you uploaded both HSLC and Higher Secondary records. |
| Employment Exchange registration card | Match the registration number to your application. |
| Caste certificate, if applicable | Bring the original issued by a competent authority authorised by the Government of Assam. |
| Other certificates you claimed | Bring the relevant originals for Home Guard, VDP, Civil Defence, SPO, FMMO, sports or NCC claims. Section 6.2.B lists which claims each certificate supports. |
| Photocopies | Make one self-attested set of the certificates required under section 6.2. Arrange it in the same order as the originals. |

## Check claims against the submitted application

Open the application acknowledgement and compare the name, date of birth, post, category and Employment Exchange number against the original documents. If you claimed both a quota and age relaxation, check the respective certificate requirements separately. For example, the notice specifies different experience periods for a Home Guard quota claim and a Home Guard age-relaxation claim.

The original advertisement says an applicant unable to show the original caste certificate at PET/PST would be treated as General. It also says later submission after PET/PST would be refused. Check the [Constable recruitment guide]({{base}}/jobs/assam-police-constable-recruitment/) for the corrigendum and instructions for your cycle. Then review the [PET and PST standards]({{base}}/assam-police-constable-pst-pet/) for your branch.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are copies enough for Assam Police document verification?

The advertisement requires the original uploaded documents plus one self-attested copy set.

### Should I take both my marksheet and pass certificate?

Yes, where the qualification applies to your post. Section 6.2 lists both for HSLC and Higher Secondary as applicable.

### Can I submit a missing certificate after PET and PST?

The advertisement says documents will not be accepted after PET and PST. Check the latest instructions for your own recruitment.
