**For an Assistant Professor application, start with the vacancy notice and match it to the certificate or result category you hold.** The SLET Commission's 2025–26 brochure describes Assistant Professor eligibility within its member-state jurisdiction. The National Testing Agency (NTA) lists several UGC NET outcomes, so the exact category on your result matters.

## What to compare

| Question | SLET North East | UGC NET |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who publishes the rules? | SLET Commission, Assam (N.E. Region) | National Testing Agency in the UGC NET information bulletin |
| What do the named sources describe? | A State Eligibility Test for Assistant Professor eligibility within the Commission's member states | Results for JRF with Assistant Professor eligibility, Assistant Professor with PhD admission, or PhD admission only |
| What should you check for a vacancy? | Subject, certificate, jurisdiction and the recruitment notice's wording | Subject and whether your result category includes Assistant Professor eligibility |

Keep the current test bulletin and the recruitment advertisement together when you check your eligibility. They answer different questions. The bulletin explains the test and result categories. The advertisement states which qualifications the hiring authority accepts for that post.

## Check your certificate before filling the form

1. Copy the advertised subject, post and accepted qualifications from the notice.
2. Match those fields to the wording on your certificate or scorecard, including the result category for UGC NET.
3. Check any subject-equivalence, jurisdiction or exemption wording in the advertisement and applicable rules.
4. Save the certificate and the records that support the category you claim. See the [Assam Assistant Professor application checklist]({{base}}/assam-assistant-professor-application-checklist/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a UGC NET result for PhD admission alone include Assistant Professor eligibility?

The NTA lists PhD admission only as a separate result category. Check that your result expressly includes Assistant Professor eligibility for applications that require it.

### Where does a North East SLET certificate apply?

The SLET Commission brochure describes eligibility within the jurisdiction of its member states. Check the applicable bulletin and the vacancy notice for the post you want.

### Is a test certificate the entire Assistant Professor application?

The certificate is one item. The vacancy notice governs the other educational qualifications, subject requirements and documents you must submit.
