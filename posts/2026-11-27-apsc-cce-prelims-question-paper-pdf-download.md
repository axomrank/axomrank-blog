## Choose the right booklet

Choose GS I for General Studies and GS II for CSAT in the cycles with both booklets. Read the instructions on each file because the scheme can differ across years. The 2023–2025 papers have online practice links and matching key PDFs where listed. The [CSAT syllabus and practice guide]({{base}}/apsc-csat-syllabus-practice/) explains the available transcriptions and how to use them.
