**For an EWS claim, start with the recruiting authority's notice.** For an Assam state post, check whether the notice offers an Economically Weaker Sections (EWS) reservation and specifies a certificate. For a central post, follow the central recruitment notice's prescribed Income and Asset Certificate format, income year and issue-date rules. Ask the issuing office for the format specified by the recruiter.

## Which EWS certificate should you request?

| Where you are applying | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Government of Assam post | The vacancy's reservation table, any referenced Personnel Department order and the certificate instructions. Assam's December 2022 order put EWS reservation for state direct recruitment on hold. Read the current advertisement and corrigenda before making a claim. |
| Central government post | The Income and Asset Certificate annexure in the notice, the competent issuing authority, the income year, any stated validity year and any deadline for issuance. |

Assam's Personnel Department identifies the Circle Officer or Circle Officer (Attached) as the competent authority to issue Income and Asset Certificates. When you contact the Revenue Circle office, have the recruitment notice and annexure ready so you can specify the purpose, format and year. The Assam Gazette memorandum of 29 April 2023 reiterates that an EWS certificate issued in a particular financial year is valid for that financial year.

## What the two financial years mean

The **income year** is the period used to assess income. The **validity year**, when specified, is the year the certificate is valid for under the notice. An **issue-date window** sets when the authority must have issued the certificate. Copy these requirements from your recruitment notice separately.

| Published example | What the notice specifies |
| --- | --- |
| SSC Combined Graduate Level Examination, 2026 | The certificate must be valid for FY 2026–27 and based on income for FY 2025–26. See sections 6.4 and 6.6 and the format in Annexure XI. |
| UPSC Civil Services Examination, 2026 | The notice uses income for FY 2024–25 and requires a certificate issued on or after 1 April 2025 and by the applicable application deadline. Read the notice with any deadline extension or other corrigendum. |

## Check the certificate before you submit

1. Open the notice's EWS clause and annexure. Note whether the post offers a reservation, which authority can sign, and which dates apply.
2. Bring the annexure when you contact the issuing office. Ask which family income and asset records are needed for your application.
3. Compare the issued certificate with the annexure. Check your name, financial year, issue date and the issuing officer's designation. Save the certificate with the vacancy notice and your acknowledgement.

Use the [Assam government job documents checklist]({{base}}/assam-government-job-documents-checklist/) to prepare the rest of your application.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can an EWS certificate issued in Assam be used for a central job?

Check whether it meets the central recruiter's format, issuing authority and date conditions. For SSC CGL 2026, the notice calls for the format in Annexure XI.

### Is an ordinary income certificate enough for an EWS claim?

Where the vacancy requires an Income and Asset Certificate for EWS, use that prescribed certificate, which addresses both income and assets.

### Can I reuse last year's EWS certificate?

Compare the income year, validity year and issue date on the certificate against the new notice. Request the prescribed certificate if the existing one falls outside those conditions.
