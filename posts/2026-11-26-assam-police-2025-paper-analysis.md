## How to practise

1. Start with the paper for your own post. Answer without opening the answer panels. Keep a list of guesses as well as mistakes.
2. Sort the misses by subject. SI readers can start by reviewing the history items and then testing the shared maths and reasoning topics. Constable readers can give English equal time alongside maths and reasoning.
3. Follow the links for [Assam history]({{base}}/questions/assam-history/), [number system]({{base}}/questions/number-system/) and [grammar]({{base}}/questions/grammar/). Try fresh questions in the categories you missed.
4. Retake the marked questions before starting the other booklet. Compare the mistakes that repeat across both.
