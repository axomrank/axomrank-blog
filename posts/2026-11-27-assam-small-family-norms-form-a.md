**Open Form A in the Assam Gazette if your government recruitment notice asks for a Small Family Norms declaration.** The declaration records the post and advertisement you are applying against and the information required under the cited rules. If the recruitment supplies its own copy, use the one attached to that notice.

[Open the official Form A page in the Gazette PDF](https://www.aegcl.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/010720211600_SMALL_FAMILY_NORM.pdf#page=5)

## Check these fields before signing

| Part of the form | What to compare |
| --- | --- |
| Applicant details | Use the name, address and personal details that belong to the applicant. Compare them with the recruitment application. |
| Post and advertisement | Copy the exact post name and advertisement reference from the issuing authority. |
| Family particulars | Complete the applicable fields truthfully using the official form's instructions. Read the rule and its provisions if you are unsure how a field applies. |
| Place, date and signature | Complete the signing section and confirm that all the fields and the signature are readable in the uploaded file. |

The Gazette includes both the eligibility rule and its provisions. Read the full text and current recruitment instructions when applying it to your circumstances. The rule assigns interpretation questions to the Assam Personnel Department.

## Where Form A goes in an APSC application

The **CCE 2025 advertisement, No. 01/2026**, listed Form A in its Appendix I document uploads. Open the advertisement's upload instructions, prepare the signed declaration in the format it requests, and check the uploaded file in the application preview before filing. Save the submitted application and the exact copy you uploaded.

See the [Assam Public Service Commission registration guide]({{base}}/apsc-one-time-registration-assam/) for the steps between creating a profile and submitting a post application.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where can I download the Assam Small Family Norms declaration?

Form A appears on the final PDF page of the Assam Gazette copy hosted by AEGCL. Check whether your recruitment notice supplies a separate copy of the form.

### Can I reuse a Form A from an older application?

The Gazette form identifies the post and advertisement. Check those fields for the vacancy you are applying to and complete the declaration with the correct current details.

### What should I check if the notice also asks for an affidavit?

Read the document checklist and match each requested upload to its named form or affidavit. Use the recruitment instructions for the signing and attestation requirements of each document.
