The Ahom kingdom ruled the Brahmaputra valley for about six centuries, from **Chaolung Sukaphaa in 1228** to **Purandar Singha in 1838**. Questions on Ahom kings appear in ADRE, APSC CCE, Assam TET and Assam Police papers almost every year.

Below is the full list of **41 reigns**. Each name links to a free, short bilingual lesson on that king.

## Why do some lists say 39 kings and others 41?

Two kings ruled twice. **Chandrakanta Singha** (1811–1818 and 1819–1821) and **Purandar Singha** (1818–1819 and 1833–1838) each appear two times. Counting **reigns** gives 41. Counting **people** gives 39. If an exam option says "41", it is counting reigns.

Also note two dates that are easy to mix up:

- **1826**: the Treaty of Yandabo. The Burmese invasions ended and the British took control of Assam.
- **1838**: Purandar Singha's second reign, a restoration under the British in Upper Assam, ended when the British deposed him and annexed his territory.

## The five eras at a glance

- **The founding kings (1228–1497):** Sukaphaa crosses the Patkai and settles at Charaideo. The kingdom slowly grows.
- **Expansion (1497–1603):** Suhungmung, the first Ahom king to take a Hindu title (Swarganarayan), expands the kingdom and fights the Turbak invasion. Mula Gabharu dies in battle.
- **Against the Mughals (1603–1681):** Pratap Singha's wars, Mir Jumla's invasion under Jayadhwaj Singha, Chakradhwaj Singha appointing Lachit Barphukan (1667) to retake Guwahati, and the Battle of Saraighat (March 1671) under Udayaditya Singha.
- **The Tungkhungia kings (1681–1769):** Gadadhar Singha drives the Mughals out of Guwahati at Itakhuli (1682). Rudra Singha builds Rangpur, and Rang Ghar is built under Pramatta Singha.
- **Decline and the last kings (1769–1838):** the Moamoria rebellion, Burmese invasions and British annexation.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who was the first Ahom king?

Chaolung Sukaphaa, who founded the kingdom in 1228 and ruled until 1268. His first capital was Charaideo.

### Who was the last Ahom king?

Purandar Singha. His second reign (1833–1838) was the last Ahom reign before the British annexed his territory in 1838.

### How many Ahom kings were there?

There were 41 reigns by 39 people, because Chandrakanta Singha and Purandar Singha each ruled twice.

### How long did the Ahom kingdom last?

From 1228 to 1838, about 600 years.
