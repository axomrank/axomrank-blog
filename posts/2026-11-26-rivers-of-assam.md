For Assam exam questions, start with the **Brahmaputra and Barak river systems**. Then group Brahmaputra tributaries by the bank where they join. For example, **Manas** joins from the north and **Dikhow** from the south. Both distinctions matter in previous-year papers.

## Examples to learn first

| Group | Rivers to place on the map |
| --- | --- |
| Brahmaputra main channel | Flows generally east to west across the Assam valley |
| North-bank tributaries | Subansiri, Jia Bharali, Manas, Beki and Sankosh |
| South-bank tributaries | Burhi Dihing, Disang, Dikhow, Dhansiri (South), Kopili and Kulsi |
| Barak system | Barak and its lower branches, Surma and Kushiyara |

The Water Resources Department lists both a North Dhansiri and a South Dhansiri. Keep the qualifier when you write your notes. Kopili also appears as *Kapili* or *Kopilli* in source material.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which are the two main river systems of Assam?

Assam's Water Resources Department describes the Brahmaputra and Barak river systems.

### Does the Dikhow join the north or south bank?

The Dikhow is a south-bank tributary of the Brahmaputra.

### Is the Manas a north-bank tributary?

Yes. The Water Resources Department lists Manas among the north-bank tributaries.
