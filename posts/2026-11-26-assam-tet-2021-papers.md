Choose the booklet for your exam level before you start. Assam held a **Higher Secondary TET** for postgraduate teachers in January 2021. Samagra Shiksha Axom also published a separate **General TET 2021** notice and syllabus for Lower Primary and Upper Primary. The booklets below cover Higher Secondary and Special TET.

## What you can practise

| Booklet | Questions available | Where to start |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| [Higher Secondary TET Paper I]({{base}}/question-papers/assam-tet-2021-paper-i-question-paper/) | 100 | Use it for general-paper practice and check the booklet label GHT-21. |
| [Higher Secondary TET Paper II, Commerce]({{base}}/question-papers/assam-tet-2021-paper-ii-question-paper/) | 100 | Use the OHT-21 Option A booklet for Commerce topics. |
| [Special TET Paper I]({{base}}/question-papers/assam-tet-2021-special-paper-i-question-paper/) | 120 | The online questions cover the common sections. Use the source booklet for the optional Language I section. |

The collection contains **320 transcribed questions** across these three booklets. The answer panels distinguish AxomRank's worked solutions and editorially revised questions. Match the syllabus in your notification to the booklet you choose.

## Revise from your mistakes

Mark any question you guessed, even if you answered it correctly. For teaching-method and child-development questions, write a short reason for the option you selected. Practise the [teaching methods]({{base}}/questions/teaching-methods/) and [child development]({{base}}/questions/child-development/) topic sets before repeating the marked questions.

The [Assam TET PDF collection]({{base}}/assam-tet-question-paper-pdf-download/) also lists the available General, Higher Secondary and later Special TET scans.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which Assam TET 2021 papers are available?

This collection has Higher Secondary Paper I, Higher Secondary Paper II Option A for Commerce, and the common sections of Special TET Paper I.

### Where can I get the official 2021 General TET syllabus?

Samagra Shiksha Axom publishes the [General TET 2021 Lower Primary and Upper Primary syllabi](https://ssa.assam.gov.in/latest/tet-online-apply-with-syllabus-2021).

### Does the Special TET practice page include Language I?

The practice page covers questions 1–120. Consult the linked source booklet for the optional Language I questions.
