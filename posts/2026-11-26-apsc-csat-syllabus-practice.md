**APSC CSAT is Prelims General Studies Paper II.** The published syllabus lists 80 questions, 200 marks and two hours. It covers comprehension, communication, reasoning, decision-making, general mental ability, basic numeracy and data interpretation.

## Match the syllabus to practice

| Syllabus area | What to practise |
| --- | --- |
| Comprehension | Find the sentence or argument in the passage that supports your choice. Try [2025 GS-II, Q2]({{base}}/question-papers/apsc-cce-prelims-2025-gs-ii-question-paper/#q2). |
| Interpersonal and communication skills | Distinguish the type or purpose of communication in the example. Try [2025 GS-II, Q23]({{base}}/question-papers/apsc-cce-prelims-2025-gs-ii-question-paper/#q23). |
| Logical reasoning and mental ability | Draw a diagram or write the relation between statements before selecting an answer. Try [2025 GS-II, Q42]({{base}}/question-papers/apsc-cce-prelims-2025-gs-ii-question-paper/#q42). |
| Decision-making and problem solving | Write the goal, constraints and likely consequences of the options. |
| Basic numeracy and data interpretation | Practise with the Class X-level numbers, tables and graphs named in the syllabus. |

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the syllabus for APSC CSAT?

It includes comprehension, communication, logical and analytical reasoning, decision-making, general mental ability, basic numeracy and data interpretation.

### How long is APSC CSAT in the published syllabus?

The APSC GS-II syllabus lists two hours for 80 questions worth 200 marks. Read the instructions for the cycle you are sitting.

### Which older papers should I use for practice?

Start with one of the linked 2024 or 2025 GS-II booklets and keep the other for a fresh attempt after revision.
