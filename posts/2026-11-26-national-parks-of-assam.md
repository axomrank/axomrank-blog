The **Economic Survey, Assam 2025–26 lists eight national parks**. Its list includes Sikhna Jwhwlao, in Kokrajhar and Chirang. For exam revision, learn the name of each park and one association you can recall under pressure.

## The eight parks in the Economic Survey

| Park | Revision cue |
| --- | --- |
| Kaziranga | Greater one-horned rhinoceros and UNESCO World Heritage status |
| Manas | UNESCO World Heritage status and the Bhutan foothills |
| Nameri | Study it with the Jia-Bhoroli River |
| Orang | Study it with the Brahmaputra's north bank |
| Dibru-Saikhowa | Study it with the floodplains around Tinsukia and Dibrugarh |
| Raimona | Kokrajhar and the golden langur |
| Dihing Patkai (Dehing Patkai) | Lowland rainforest in Dibrugarh and Tinsukia |
| Sikhna Jwhwlao | Kokrajhar and Chirang, the eighth park in the survey |

The survey uses the spelling *Sikhna Jahwlao*. Assam's preliminary notification gives *Sikhna Jwhwlao*. Use the date of the source when you encounter a different park count in an older paper.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many national parks does Assam have?

The Economic Survey, Assam 2025–26 lists eight, including Sikhna Jwhwlao.

### Which districts contain Sikhna Jwhwlao National Park?

The survey places it in Kokrajhar and Chirang. The preliminary notification names the same districts.

### Which Assam national parks are UNESCO World Heritage Sites?

Kaziranga and Manas are each listed by UNESCO. The UNESCO listing for Manas uses the name Manas Wildlife Sanctuary.
