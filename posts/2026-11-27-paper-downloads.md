## Frequently asked questions

### Which Assam previous-year question papers can I download?

The collections above cover ADRE, APSC CCE and departmental recruitment, Assam Police, teacher eligibility tests, health and forest recruitment, office posts and a B.Ed. entrance exam.

### Are answer keys available for every question paper?

Matching key links are included beside selected papers. The PDF can be downloaded independently. Use the year, paper and series shown in both files to confirm the match.

### What does the cycle year mean?

For departmental APSC recruitments it refers to the advertisement year in the archive. Check the test date printed on the PDF cover when comparing booklets.
