The APSC Combined Competitive Examination (CCE) Prelims has two papers: **GS-I**, which decides the merit list, and **GS-II (CSAT)**, which is qualifying. We took the papers for **2023, 2024 and 2025** and tagged every question we could read by subject and topic. All of it is open in the [AxomRank PYQ dataset](https://github.com/axomrank/assam-exam-pyq).

> **Method note.** The booklets are scans, so we extracted them with OCR. Every GS-I paper came out almost complete (295 of 300 questions). GS-II came out less complete (234 questions) because some items are diagrams or passages. Answers follow APSC's **official provisional keys**.

## GS-I: polity is a quarter of the paper

Across 295 GS-I questions (2023: 100, 2024: 99, 2025: 96):

| Subject | Questions | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Polity | 73 | 25% |
| Geography | 40 | 14% |
| History | 35 | 12% |
| General awareness and current affairs | 32 | 11% |
| Economy | 31 | 11% |
| Environment and ecology | 31 | 11% |
| Science | 28 | 9% |
| Art and culture | 22 | 7% |
| Computer | 3 | 1% |

Polity questions are mostly about **governance and institutions** (28) and the **Indian Constitution** (22), and **Assam administration** adds 18 more.

## About one GS-I question in four is about Assam

**80 of the 295 GS-I questions (27%)** were tagged to an Assam topic:

| Assam topic | Questions |
|---|---:|
| Assam history | 23 |
| Assam geography | 21 |
| Assam administration | 18 |
| Assam culture | 14 |
| Assam economy | 4 |

By year: 28 in 2023, 30 in 2024 and 22 in 2025. National coaching material often covers these topics only briefly, but they can decide your rank, because most candidates prepare the national syllabus well.

## GS-II (CSAT): four equal blocks

| Subject | Questions | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Reasoning | 69 | 29% |
| Aptitude (communication, decision making, data interpretation) | 68 | 29% |
| English (mostly reading comprehension) | 49 | 21% |
| Mathematics | 47 | 20% |

CSAT is qualifying, but you still have to clear the cut-off. Reading comprehension (44 questions) and logical and analytical reasoning (45) were the two largest topics.

## What to do with this

1. **Polity first.** Build the Constitution and institutions base, then add the Assam administration layer: districts, the Sixth Schedule councils and state bodies.
2. **Make Assam a separate subject.** Give Assam history, geography and culture their own weekly hours instead of treating them as a part of Indian GK.
3. **Practise CSAT from week one** with timed comprehension passages, so you are not caught out by the qualifying paper.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is APSC CSAT qualifying?

Yes. GS-II (CSAT) is a qualifying paper, and the merit list is decided by GS-I. Check the current notification for the exact cut-off percentage.

### How many Assam-specific questions come in APSC Prelims?

In 2023–2025, 80 of the 295 readable GS-I questions (about 27%) were about Assam history, geography, administration, culture or economy.

### Which subject has the most questions in APSC GS-I?

Polity, with 73 of 295 questions (25%) across 2023–2025.
