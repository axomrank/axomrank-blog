Most "ADRE syllabus" pages list subjects. Few show how many marks each subject has actually carried. We extracted **all 1,290 questions** from the ten ADRE papers SLRC Assam set in 2022 and 2024 and tagged each one by subject and topic. The full dataset is open on [GitHub](https://github.com/axomrank/assam-exam-pyq), so you can check every number below.

> **How to read this.** Counts come from the question booklets themselves. The 2024 answers follow SLRC's **official final key**. The 2022 papers had no published key, so those answers are worked solutions, with reasoning recorded for each one.

## A practical weekly split

Based on these shares, a Grade III aspirant with 10 study hours a week could try:

- **4 hours** maths and reasoning, solving timed sets rather than reading theory
- **2 hours** English vocabulary and grammar, with a short daily word list
- **2 hours** Assam GK: culture, geography and history
- **2 hours** a full previous-year paper under exam timing, then reviewing every mistake

Grade IV aspirants can move one English hour to reasoning.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many questions are in an ADRE paper?

In 2024, each Grade III paper had 150 questions and each Grade IV paper had 135. In 2022, Grade III papers had 100 questions and Grade IV papers had 135.

### Are the ADRE 2024 answer keys official?

Yes. The 2024 answers in our dataset follow SLRC Assam's official final key. The 2022 papers had no published key, so those answers are worked solutions with the reasoning recorded.

### Which subject should I start with for ADRE Grade III?

Start with maths and English. Together they were 44% of Grade III questions in 2022 and 2024.

### Where can I practise ADRE previous year questions for free?

All 1,290 questions are in the open [AxomRank PYQ dataset](https://github.com/axomrank/assam-exam-pyq), and the [AxomRank app]({{app}}/) has free bilingual lessons for the Assam history, polity and geography topics.
