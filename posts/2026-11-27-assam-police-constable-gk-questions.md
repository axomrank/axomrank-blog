## Answers and explanations

### History answers

1. **C — Lachit Borphukan.** His command is associated with the Ahom victory at Saraighat in 1671. See the [Battle of Saraighat guide]({{base}}/battle-of-saraighat/).
2. **A — Charaideo.** It was the first Tai-Ahom capital. The Charaideo moidams are the burial mounds of Ahom royalty. Review the sequence in the [Ahom kings guide]({{base}}/ahom-kings-list/).
3. **D — Watching sports and public festivities.** The Sivasagar district administration identifies Rang Ghar as a pavilion built for recreational events in the Ahom era.
4. **B — Treaty of Yandabo.** The 1826 agreement ended the First Anglo-Burmese War.
5. **B — Sattra.** Sattras are religious and cultural institutions linked to Assam's Vaishnava movement. See the [Sankardeva and sattras guide]({{base}}/sankardeva-and-sattras/).

### Geography answers

6. **C — Brahmaputra and Barak.** These form Assam's two main drainage systems. Use the [Assam rivers guide]({{base}}/rivers-of-assam/) to revise their valleys.
7. **A — Barak.** Cachar's district plan places Silchar on the Barak's south bank.
8. **D — Deepor Beel.** The freshwater wetland provides stormwater storage near Guwahati. Read the [Deepor Beel Ramsar guide]({{base}}/deepor-beel-ramsar-site/).
9. **C — Majuli.** The island landscape is shaped by channels and erosion in the Brahmaputra system.
10. **A — Kaziranga National Park.** UNESCO cites its role in conserving the Indian one-horned rhinoceros. Compare its location and habitats with the other [national parks of Assam]({{base}}/national-parks-of-assam/).

### Polity answers

11. **D — Sixth Schedule.** Read it with Article 244(2). See the [Sixth Schedule councils in Assam guide]({{base}}/sixth-schedule-councils-in-assam/).
12. **B — The Governor.** Article 164 gives the Governor the power to appoint the Chief Minister.
13. **A — The State Election Commission.** Article 243K places Panchayat electoral rolls and elections under its supervision, direction and control.
14. **D — Anchalik Panchayat.** This is the intermediate tier between the Gaon Panchayat and the Zilla Parishad under the Assam Panchayat system.
15. **C — Gaon Sabha.** The Assam Panchayat Act defines this body by the village's electoral rolls. The Gaon Panchayat is the corresponding village-level institution established under the Act.

### Economy answers

16. **B — Digboi.** The Press Information Bureau dates the refinery's commissioning to 1901.
17. **A — Sualkuchi.** Kamrup's district census handbook describes the village's Muga, Pat and Eri silk weaving.
18. **C — Bringing buyers and sellers together to trade tea.** Guwahati is one of the registered centres where Tea Board describes bulk tea being sold through public auctions.
19. **B — GSDP at constant prices.** The constant-price series is the one to use for real output growth. Compare the two series in the [Assam Economic Survey guide]({{base}}/assam-economic-survey-2025-26-for-exams/).
20. **D — Gross State Domestic Product.** It measures the value of production within the state over a period.
