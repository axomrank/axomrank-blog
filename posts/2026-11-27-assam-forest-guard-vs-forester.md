**The January 2026 SLPRB advertisement asked for Higher Secondary or equivalent for Forest Guard and a bachelor's degree or equivalent for Forester Grade I.** The two posts shared physical standards and a qualifying walking test, while their written papers used different marking schemes.

## Forest Guard and Forester Grade I compared

| Requirement in advertisement 731/2025/134 | Forest Guard | Forester Grade I |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Education | Higher Secondary (10+2) or equivalent from a recognised board or council | Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised university, or equivalent |
| Written test | 100 questions at half a mark each. No penalty for wrong answers. | 100 questions at one mark each. Each wrong answer deducts half a mark. |
| Walking test | Qualifying only | Qualifying only |

The notice lists PET, medical examination, PST, a written test, a walking test and viva for these posts. The walking test in section 7.9 is separate from the running event. For both posts, the test was **26 km for men and 16 km for women within four hours**.

Section 5.7.1 also lists a minimum normal chest measurement of 79 cm with 5 cm expansion for men. Check the criteria for your post in the advertisement and later instructions. The [Assam Police Constable standards]({{base}}/assam-police-constable-pst-pet/) come from a different advertisement.

## How to choose written practice

Forest Guard candidates can practise elementary arithmetic, English, reasoning and Assam topics from the [Assam Police question archive]({{base}}/syllabus/assam-police/) where the topics overlap. Compare the exact syllabi before using a paper. For Forester Grade I, keep a separate log of doubtful answers, since the scoring scheme deducts half a mark for each wrong response. Read the culture and history topics in section 7.8.D.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I apply for Assam Forest Guard after Class 12?

The cited notice accepts Higher Secondary or equivalent for Forest Guard. The applicant still needs to meet its remaining eligibility requirements.

### Do both posts have the same walking test?

Yes, section 7.9 gives the same distance and time conditions for the two posts. The test is qualifying and carries no marks.

### Do both written exams have negative marking?

The January 2026 advertisement deducts half a mark per wrong answer for Forester Grade I. Its Forest Guard scheme has no negative marking.
