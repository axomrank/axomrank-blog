## What to check in an APSC demo

| What you need | Evidence to ask for |
| --- | --- |
| Prelims GS-I and CSAT | A recent sample test for each paper and an explanation of how instructors review mistakes. |
| Mains feedback | An anonymised marked answer. Read the comments and check whether they explain what to change in the next attempt. |
| Assam-specific coverage | A lesson or marked answer from **GS-V**, the Assam-focused Mains paper. |
| Assamese-medium support | A lesson, a question sheet and a marked answer in the language you plan to use. Check these separately. |
| A schedule you can keep | The timetable for your batch, recording access or make-up classes, and the support available after a missed class. |

Take the same question to every demo. For example, ask the teacher to explain the north- and south-bank tributary distinction in [APSC 2024 GS-I, Q18]({{base}}/question-papers/apsc-cce-prelims-2024-gs-i-question-paper/#q18). For Mains, ask to see feedback on an Assam-specific answer that missed part of the question. Compare what each teacher helps the student do differently.

## Decide whether you need a full course

1. Attempt [APSC 2024 GS-I]({{base}}/question-papers/apsc-cce-prelims-2024-gs-i-question-paper/) and [GS-II (CSAT)]({{base}}/question-papers/apsc-cce-prelims-2024-gs-ii-question-paper/) under the time limits on the booklets.
2. Review your misses on each paper. Note which came from unfamiliar material, misreading the question, or running out of time.
3. Compare your gaps with the [APSC CCE syllabus]({{base}}/syllabus/apsc-cce/). If several core subjects are unfamiliar and you need a regular timetable, compare foundation batches. If timed practice or written feedback is the main gap, compare services that focus on it.
4. Keep the next [real APSC paper]({{base}}/question-papers/#apsc-cce-prelims) for a fresh check after your next round of revision.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which is the best APSC coaching institute in Assam?

Choose the institute that can show the APSC syllabus coverage, sample evaluation and language support you need. Check the timetable and total fee for your exact batch before enrolling.

### How much does APSC coaching cost?

Fees depend on the course scope. The listings above give ₹20,000 for a Prelims course and ₹34,999 for a foundation course covering Prelims, Mains and interview. Ask for a written quote covering the stages and services you need.

### Can I prepare for APSC CCE without coaching?

Yes. Use the official syllabus, attempt real Prelims papers under time limits, and arrange regular feedback on Mains answers. Start with those steps before deciding whether to pay for help.

### What should Assamese-medium candidates check before enrolling?

Request a sample lecture, test and evaluated answer in Assamese. Confirm the language of notes, doubt-clearing and written feedback for the batch you plan to attend.
