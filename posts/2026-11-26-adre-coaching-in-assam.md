**Choose ADRE coaching after you have tried the previous-year paper for your grade and post.** If you can solve the questions but struggle to finish, look for timed mocks with answer explanations. If you repeatedly get stuck on the same maths, reasoning or English topics, ask for a demo lesson on one of those topics before paying for a full batch.

## Use your mistakes to choose what to pay for

- **You missed the same kind of maths question repeatedly.** Ask for a demo on that topic. For example, try the [HSSLC LCM and HCF question]({{base}}/question-papers/adre-2022-grade-3-hsslc-question-paper/#q41) and ask the instructor to show why the method works.
- **You knew the method and ran out of time.** Ask for a timed full mock for your post and a sample review showing which questions took the most time.
- **You struggled with Assamese or English wording.** Ask for explanation and a test sample in your preferred language. Make sure you can follow both comfortably on the device you use.
- **You can revise independently.** Follow the [free eight-week ADRE plan]({{base}}/adre-preparation-without-coaching/) and use later papers to check progress before adding paid support.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is ADRE coaching necessary?

You can prepare with the official notice, real past papers and regular timed practice. A batch may help if you need a timetable, explanations for weak topics or someone to review your mistakes.

### How much does ADRE coaching cost?

The two online examples above list ₹2,999 and ₹3,999. Ask for the full fee and confirm the tests, materials and feedback included in the exact batch you plan to join.

### Is online or classroom ADRE coaching better?

Choose the format you can attend consistently and check how you get feedback after tests. Online batches can reduce travel. Classroom batches can be useful if in-person questions and a fixed schedule help you study.

### What should a useful ADRE mock include?

Look for a timed test at the correct grade and post level, an answer key and explanations for missed questions. Compare a sample with an actual ADRE booklet.
