**Archived recruitment notice.** The State Level Police Recruitment Board (SLPRB) Assam advertised **48 Sub Inspector (UB) posts** in Assam Police on 12 December 2025. The February 2026 corrigendum revised the upper age limit for the SI (UB) post and reopened applications from 15 to 28 February 2026. Those dates have passed. Check the [official board website](https://slprbassam.in/) for new notices.

## Tentative vacancies in the original advertisement

| Post | Department | Posts |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Sub Inspector (UB) | Assam Police | 48 |
| Sub Inspector (Communication) | Assam Police Radio Organisation | 4 |
| Station Officer | Fire & Emergency Services | 6 |
| Squad Commander | Fire & Emergency Services | 5 |
| Assistant Jailor | Prison Department | 39 |

The advertisement describes these vacancies as tentative. It is separate from the recruitment notice for SI (AB) in the Assam Commando Battalions, which the February corrigendum also covers.

## SI (UB) eligibility and dates

For SI (UB), the original advertisement required a graduate degree in Arts, Science, Commerce or an equivalent stream from an eligible recognised institution. The corrigendum raised the upper age limit for the unreserved category from 26 to 27 years and updated the corresponding upper ages for reserved categories. Read the original eligibility section and corrigendum together for category-specific rules.

| Item | Dates or fee |
| --- | --- |
| Original application window | 16 December 2025 to 16 January 2026 |
| Reopened window in the 13 February corrigendum | 15 to 28 February 2026 |
| Application fee stated in the advertisement | None |
