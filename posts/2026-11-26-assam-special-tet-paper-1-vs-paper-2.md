**Paper I covers Lower Primary classes I–V, while Paper II covers Upper Primary classes VI–VIII.** This guide summarises the Assam Special TET advertisement issued in January 2026 for Bodo, Garo, Manipuri and Hmar mediums. Use the official syllabus PDFs for your medium and paper.

## Compare the papers from the official notice

| Component | Paper I | Paper II |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Child Development and Pedagogy | 30 marks | 30 marks |
| Language I, one of the listed languages | 30 marks | 30 marks |
| Language II, English | 30 marks | 30 marks |
| Mathematics and Environmental Studies | 30 marks each | See the subject option below |
| Mathematics and Science or Social Science | See the Paper I subjects above | 60 marks for the selected option |
| Total | 150 marks | 150 marks |

Open the [official Special TET 2026 portal](https://sebamis.sebaonline.org/spltet2026/). Download the Language I PDF for your chosen language and the English and pedagogy PDFs for your paper. For Paper II, also download the document for your subject option.

## Frequently asked questions

### What distinguishes Assam Special TET Paper I and Paper II?

Paper I is for Lower Primary and includes Mathematics and Environmental Studies. Paper II is for Upper Primary and has a 60-mark subject option in Mathematics and Science or Social Science.

### Which Language I choices were in the 2026 Special TET notice?

The advertisement listed Bodo, Garo, Manipuri and Hmar. Candidates selected the applicable Language I option.

### Where can I download the complete syllabus?

ASSEB Division I links the official subject PDFs for Paper I and Paper II on its Special TET 2026 portal.
