**For ADRE Grade 4 preparation, choose the Class 8 or HSLC study track according to the category of the post you applied for.** The State Level Recruitment Commission's July 2024 notice listed the same five subject areas for both tracks and set different school levels for the questions. Use that notice as a historical reference and check the instructions for your own recruitment when they are available.

## Class 8 and HSLC preparation at a glance

| Track in the 2024 notice | School level used in the notice | Where to begin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Read up to Class VIII | SCERT elementary-school pass level | Revise fractions, percentages and units. Practise finding a stated detail in a short passage and solving one-step reasoning problems. |
| HSLC or equivalent, including the specified HSLC-plus-ITI category | HSLC level under the Board of Secondary Education, Assam | Revise the same foundations, then work on multi-step arithmetic, passage inference and reasoning questions that combine more than one clue. |

The five subject areas were English, Social Studies, Mathematics, General Knowledge, and Mental Ability and Logical Reasoning. The notice set **135 multiple-choice questions in 150 minutes**, at one mark per correct answer with **0.25 deducted for a wrong answer**. It listed Assamese, Bengali, English, Boro and Hindi as question-paper languages. These are details of the 2024 notice linked below.

## Try the same skills at both levels

These examples show how the demands of a question can change even when the topic stays the same.

### Mathematics

**Class 8 practice:** A shop has 160 notebooks and sells 25% of them. How many notebooks does it sell?

**Answer:** 40. One quarter of 160 is 40.

**HSLC practice:** A shop sells 25% of its stock in the morning and 20% of the remaining stock in the afternoon. If 96 notebooks remain, how many did it start with?

**Answer:** 160. After the morning sale, 75% remains. After the afternoon sale, 80% of that amount remains. Multiply 0.75 by 0.80 to get 0.60. The remaining 96 notebooks are 60% of the starting stock, so divide 96 by 0.60.

### English

Read this practice passage. “The village library usually closes at four. On market day it stays open until six so people can return their books after shopping.”

**Class 8 practice:** When does the library close on market day?

**Answer:** At six. The second sentence states the time.

**HSLC practice:** What would probably happen if the library kept its usual closing time on market day?

**Answer:** Some shoppers would have less time to return their books after shopping. The passage explains what the extra opening hours allow people to do.

## A two-week study plan

| Days | Class 8 track | HSLC track |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–4 | Work through one school-level arithmetic skill and one short English passage each day. Write the step that led to each answer. | Take a short mixed diagnostic. Rework every error involving fractions, percentages, grammar or an inference from a passage. |
| 5–8 | Use a simple timeline or map for Social Studies and GK. Finish with short sequences or direction questions. | Explain each Social Studies fact in a sentence. Pair map revision with multi-clue reasoning and multi-step arithmetic. |
| 9–14 | Alternate short timed sets with untimed correction. Retake missed questions the next day. | Build up to a cycle-length mock if your current instructions give one. Track accuracy by subject and revisit the weakest area. |

Mark each mistake as a knowledge gap, a missed instruction or a timing problem. If the marking rule matches the July 2024 notice, 84 correct and 16 incorrect answers would yield 80 marks before any other adjustments. Use the scoring rule in your own notice when reviewing a timed set.

## Frequently asked questions

### Did the Class 8 and HSLC ADRE Grade 4 tracks have different subject names?

The July 2024 notice gave both tracks the same five subject areas. It tied them to different school levels.

### Which Grade 4 track should I prepare for?

Match your study track to the category for the post in your application and the instructions in your notice or call letter. The July 2024 notice grouped the specified HSLC-plus-ITI category with the HSLC track.

### Was there negative marking in the 2024 notice?

Yes. It specified a deduction of 0.25 mark for each incorrect answer. Read the rules for your recruitment before using that deduction in a mock.
