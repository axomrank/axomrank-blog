**Start with the job you want and the qualification you already hold.** Look at ADRE for Grade III and IV posts, APSC CCE for civil services, and SLPRB for Assam Police posts. For teaching, check the route for your school level or college subject. For healthcare and technical posts, start with the department's own advertisement.

## How the selection routes differ

- **ADRE and departmental recruitment.** ADRE is run by the State Level Recruitment Commissions (SLRC) for notified Grade III and IV posts. Departments can also recruit through their own notices. The Directorate of Medical Education (DME), for example, publishes separate Grade III technical and non-technical notices. The same grade label can lead to a different syllabus and selection process.
- **APSC CCE and police recruitment.** APSC CCE candidates prepare for objective Prelims, descriptive Mains and an interview. SLPRB candidates should check the written, physical and medical stages listed for their own post. SLPRB also publishes advertisements for posts such as Forest Guard and Fireman, with requirements specific to those jobs.
- **School and college teaching.** Passing a Teacher Eligibility Test (TET) can satisfy an eligibility requirement. You still have to apply to a teacher recruitment and meet its other conditions. The Assam Gazette provides for the Assam College Services Recruitment Board (ACSRB) to select and recommend candidates for covered college posts. For academic eligibility, check the subject-specific notice and the [SLET North East and UGC NET guide]({{base}}/slet-ne-vs-ugc-net/).

## How to prepare for more than one Assam exam

1. **Choose a primary exam and a backup with overlapping topics.** If both syllabi include arithmetic or reasoning, practise those together. Use [Assam GK]({{base}}/assam-gk/) and [monthly current affairs]({{base}}/current-affairs/) for the Assam topics on your syllabi.
2. **Attempt a paper at your own level.** Try [ADRE HSSLC]({{base}}/question-papers/adre-2024-grade-3-hsslc-question-paper/), [APSC Prelims GS-I]({{base}}/question-papers/apsc-cce-prelims-2025-gs-i-question-paper/) or [Assam Police Constable]({{base}}/question-papers/assam-police-constable-2025-question-paper/) under the original paper's time limit. Mark mistakes as knowledge gaps, calculation errors or time pressure. Start the next session with the section where you lost the most marks.
3. **Practise for the stages unique to your target.** For APSC, make time for [CSAT]({{base}}/apsc-csat-syllabus-practice/) and [Mains answer writing]({{base}}/apsc-gs-v-assam-answer-writing/). For police, check the [PST and PET standards]({{base}}/assam-police-constable-pst-pet/) early. For TET, practise pedagogy and the language paper for your level. For technical jobs, give the subjects named in the departmental syllabus a separate study block.

## What to check before you apply

- Match the post name and advertisement number to the latest correction or reopening notice. AxomRank's [recruitment notices]({{base}}/jobs/) link to the issuing boards.
- Read the age and qualification cut-off dates, accepted equivalents, reservation rules, and any residence, language or Employment Exchange requirements for your post.
- Save the official advertisement, syllabus and application receipt. Keep certificates ready only as required by the notice, including any professional registration, teaching credential or driving licence.

Use the [exam directory]({{base}}/exams/) to find syllabi and papers after choosing your route.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which Assam government exams can I apply for after Class 12?

Start with HSSLC-level ADRE Grade III posts and eligible Constable posts. The December 2025 Assam Police Unarmed Branch advertisement accepted HSSLC. Check the current notice for age, physical requirements and any additional qualification.

### What is the difference between ADRE and APSC CCE?

ADRE recruits for notified Grade III and IV posts at different qualification levels. APSC CCE is a degree-level route into the Assam Civil Service and allied services, with Prelims, Mains and an interview.

### Does passing Assam TET give me a government teaching job?

Passing TET can meet an eligibility requirement. You must separately apply for a teaching post and meet the advertised professional, academic and other requirements.

### Can I prepare for ADRE and Assam Police together?

Yes. Share practice for the arithmetic, reasoning and Assam topics that appear in both syllabi. Use separate past papers and reserve time for any police physical tests and post-specific stages.
