## Choose the right booklet

For Grade III, the levels in the archive are Paper 3 (HSSLC), Paper 4 (Graduate) and Paper 5 (Driver). Grade IV Paper 1 is HSLC and Paper 2 is Class VIII. ## Frequently asked questions

### Which ADRE PDF should I choose for an HSSLC-level Grade III post?

Choose Grade III Paper 3 (HSSLC) for the year you want. The Grade IV HSLC booklet is a different paper.

### Can I download an answer key for the 2024 papers?

Yes. The archive puts the matching final-key PDF next to each listed 2024 booklet. Match the booklet series before scoring.
