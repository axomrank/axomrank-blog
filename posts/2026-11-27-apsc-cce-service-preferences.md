**List the services and posts you would actually accept in your preferred order on the APSC CCE Mains application.** In the advertisement for CCE 2025, the Assam Public Service Commission said it would consider final rank, the choices in the Mains application and applicable reservations when allotting candidates. It also said a candidate would be recommended only for a post they had opted for.

## Read the service list for your cycle

| Example from CCE 2025, advertisement 01/2026 | What to confirm before putting it on your list |
| --- | --- |
| Assam Civil Service (Junior Grade), or ACS | Look up the cadre rules and the kinds of postings that interest you. Decide whether you want a general administration route. |
| Assam Police Service (Junior Grade), or APS | Read the service rules and verify any applicable physical or medical requirements. Consider the kind of field responsibilities you are prepared to take on. |
| District Transport Officer | Check the duties and department rather than relying on the post's placement in somebody else's list. |
| Other advertised posts | Check the exact designation, eligibility and reservation details in the notice. The list can change by cycle. |

## Put your preferences in writing

1. Copy the exact service or post names from the advertisement and identify any additional conditions you need to verify.
2. For each post, write the duties that interest you and anything you would need to confirm about postings or work.
3. Rank the posts you would genuinely accept. If an eligible applicant would choose District Transport Officer ahead of APS, that order represents the applicant's priorities. It does not predict allotment.
4. Compare the worksheet to the order in your completed online application and save the acknowledgement.

## Frequently asked questions

### At which stage do I enter APSC CCE service preferences?

For CCE 2025 the Commission's advertisement specifies the preferences expressed in the Mains application. Use the current Mains instructions for your cycle.

### Do Prelims marks count toward service allotment?

The cited advertisement treats Prelims as screening. Final ranking uses Mains written and interview marks. Allotment considers rank, opted preferences and applicable reservations.

### Can APSC recommend me for an unselected post?

The CCE 2025 advertisement says it will recommend candidates only for posts they opted for.
