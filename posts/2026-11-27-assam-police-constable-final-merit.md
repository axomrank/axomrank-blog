**The December 2025 SLPRB advertisement set different final merit totals for Constable UB, male AB and female AB.** UB was scored out of **100**, male AB out of **115**, and female AB out of **95**. Use this breakdown for advertisement 727/2025/94 and check any later corrigenda that apply to your recruitment.

## Which stages count toward the final merit score?

| Component | UB, men and women | AB, men | AB, women |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Physical Efficiency Test (PET) | 40 | 60 | 40 |
| Written test | 50 | 50 | 50 |
| Viva | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| Higher Secondary marks converted to weightage | 5 | — | — |
| **Maximum** | **100** | **115** | **95** |

PST carried zero marks. The extra male AB PET marks came from chin-ups. For event rules and qualifying standards, see the [PET and PST guide]({{base}}/assam-police-constable-pst-pet/).

For **UB**, the advertisement lists **2 marks** for Higher Secondary percentages from 45 to 59.99, **3 marks** from 60 to 74.99, and **5 marks** from 75 upwards. The table starts at 45 percent.

## Two worked examples

- **UB example.** A candidate with 32 PET marks, 78 correct written answers, 4 viva marks and a Higher Secondary score of 72% gets **32 + 39 + 4 + 3 = 78 out of 100**.
- **Male AB example.** A candidate with 46 PET marks, 78 correct written answers and 4 viva marks gets **46 + 39 + 4 = 89 out of 115**.

These are illustrative scores. The advertisement awarded half a mark for each correct written answer and specified no negative marking. Work through the [Constable written paper and practice plan]({{base}}/assam-police-constable-written-exam-preparation/) to estimate your written score under that scheme.

## Shortlisting and ties

The viva shortlist in section 7.9 uses the PET and written scores together. The final merit table then adds the components applicable to the branch. For equal final marks, the advertisement places the older applicant higher. If the applicants also share a date of birth, greater height breaks the tie.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do Higher Secondary marks count for Assam Police Constable AB?

The academic weightage in section 8 applies to UB. Both AB final merit tables omit it.

### Is the physical standard test scored?

PST checks eligibility measurements and carries zero marks. The separate PET contributes to merit.

### Can I use these examples as a cut-off?

The examples show the arithmetic. Use an official published result for any actual cut-off and match the cycle, post and category.
