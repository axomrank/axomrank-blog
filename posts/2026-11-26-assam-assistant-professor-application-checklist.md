**Start with the exact college or department advertisement.** Use the form and submission channel specified by the authority for your vacancy, and map each eligibility claim to the document that supports it. The Directorate of Higher Education (DHE), Assam publishes selection guidance. Tangla College has also published an example of an Assistant Professor form for provincialised colleges.

## Assemble the documents by claim

| Claim or field | What to check and keep ready |
| --- | --- |
| Post and discipline | Save the advertisement, corrigendum, subject and category as printed. |
| Academic record | Match the required degree and subject to your marksheets and degree or provisional certificate. |
| Test qualification | Keep the NET, SET or SLET record if you are relying on it. Verify the subject and result category. |
| PhD, experience or equivalence | Provide the prescribed evidence if you claim a qualification or exemption that depends on it. |
| Category or other benefits | Confirm which certificates are accepted and their applicable date or validity. |
| Application and proof of submission | Use the required form and channel. Keep your submitted copy and its acknowledgement or dispatch record. |

Review the [SLET vs UGC NET guide]({{base}}/slet-ne-vs-ugc-net/) if you are unsure which result category supports your application. If an advertisement asks for a different supporting format, follow its own instructions.

## Verify who receives the application

Start with the issuer named in the current vacancy. Match the form and return address or online submission link to that notice, then check which certifying authority it names for any supporting records. For the board's statutory role, see the [ACSRB recruitment guide]({{base}}/acsrb-recruitment-assam/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is the Assam Assistant Professor application form?

The vacancy advertisement identifies the applicable form or online procedure. Use the Tangla College form linked below as an example of the records a college may request.

### Which documents should I compare with the recruitment notice?

Start with your degree and marksheets, any test or qualification certificate you are relying on, and relevant category and experience records. Use the notice to decide which must be submitted.

### Should I use the same form for every college vacancy?

Read the submission instructions for each vacancy. Colleges or departments may direct applicants to a particular form or application channel.
