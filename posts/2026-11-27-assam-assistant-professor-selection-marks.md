**Keep your eligibility check separate from your selection score.** A qualifying degree or certificate helps establish whether you may apply. If the college uses the cited Assam Higher Education Department guidelines, its scoring table then puts qualifying exam results into bands and awards selection marks. Match the guideline and any amendment to the exact college advertisement.

## Find the postgraduate band in the applicable memorandum

The Higher Education Department's October 2024 memorandum partially revised the January 2022 guideline for selection of Assistant Professors and Librarians in covered colleges. It changed the postgraduate calculation and retained the other conditions of the earlier memorandum.

| Postgraduate result in the October 2024 memorandum | Marks awarded in this component |
| --- | ---: |
| Above 80% | 30 |
| 60% through 80% | 27 |
| From 55% to below 60%, or from 50% where the listed relaxation applies | 24 |

Keep the university's grade-conversion evidence where the advertisement asks for it. Verify subject, eligibility and category conditions directly in the notice before interpreting a score band.

## Worked academic-record example

Suppose an applicant has **78% in HSLC, 82% in HSSLC, 72% in graduation and 74% in postgraduate study**. These are invented inputs to show the calculation. The January 2022 tables give the first three results **8, 8 and 17**. The October 2024 postgraduate amendment gives the fourth result **27**. The academic-record subtotal is **60 out of 72**. The other parts of selection follow the applicable memorandum and advertisement.

The 2022 memorandum allocated the remaining marks to research contribution and teaching experience (22), extra-curricular activity (3) and the selection committee interview (3). Keep a copy of both memoranda and look for later changes or institution-specific instructions before estimating a final score.

## What to prepare for the interview

Prepare a short explanation of your research and a sample lesson from the advertised subject. Practise answering one follow-up that checks your reasoning. Label this as preparation in your notes and follow the call letter for the actual format. Use the [Assistant Professor application checklist]({{base}}/assam-assistant-professor-application-checklist/) to organise evidence, and the [ACSRB overview]({{base}}/acsrb-recruitment-assam/) to distinguish the board’s legal role from a college advertisement.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does my postgraduate percentage equal my selection marks?

The cited memorandum converts the percentage to a band. For example, 74% earns 27 postgraduate component marks under the October 2024 revision.

### Which band takes exactly 80%?

The revised middle band covers 60% through 80%. The higher band is for results above 80%.

### Is this a new ACSRB scoring notice?

It is a worked example using Higher Education Department memoranda for covered college recruitment. Read a future ACSRB advertisement and its instructions for that intake.
