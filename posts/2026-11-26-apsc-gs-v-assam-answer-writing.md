**APSC General Studies V is the Assam-focused Mains paper.** The official syllabus labels it Paper VI and assigns 250 marks. Build notes that pair each syllabus term with an Assam example and a reliable source. Then practise using that evidence to answer what the question actually asks.

## Worked outline for a flood question

**Practice prompt:** Explain how Assam's flood preparedness can protect vulnerable households and their livelihoods.

1. **State the problem locally.** Explain how flooding can interrupt work, schooling and access to services in exposed communities. Place the example in a named river basin or district.
2. **Match actions to the stage.** Before flooding, pair forecasts and warnings with evacuation planning. During flooding, discuss rescue, shelter and access to essentials. For recovery, explain how restoring roads or services helps livelihoods resume.
3. **Name who acts.** Use the Assam State Disaster Management Authority's guidance on warnings and emergency response. Note the local authority responsible for carrying out the action where your example applies.
4. **Close with a test of delivery.** A useful conclusion says how to tell whether a measure reached vulnerable households, such as whether warnings reached the affected settlement in time.

Practise once with your notes, then rewrite from memory. On review, underline every Assam-specific example and check that each paragraph answers the prompt.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is APSC GS-V?

It is the Assam-focused General Studies paper in the Mains syllabus. The official document labels it Paper VI.

### How many marks is APSC GS-V in the published syllabus?

The official syllabus assigns 250 marks to General Studies V.

### How should I prepare Assam examples for Mains?

Build notes around official syllabus headings. For each example, keep a place, a source and a sentence explaining why it answers a question.
