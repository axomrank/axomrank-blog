**Start at the [Assam Employment Exchange service on Sewa Setu](https://sewasetu.assam.gov.in/site/service-apply/registration-of-employment-seeker-in-employment-exchange).** Choose new registration if you have never had a card, renewal if your digital card is due, or the re-registration service if you need to digitise an older paper card. Keep your current card handy so you can choose the right route.

## Choose the service that matches your card

| Your situation | What to select | What to keep ready |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You are applying for the first time | Registration of an employment seeker | Proof of residence and age, educational records and supporting certificates for any claim |
| You have a digital card and want to renew it | Renewal of a registration card | The registration number and registration date from the existing card |
| You have an older paper card that needs digitising or renewing | Re-registration, then the paper-card option shown on the form | The old card and the supporting documents the service asks for |

The state's user manual describes those branches. It also includes re-registration options for changes such as a new qualification or a transfer. If a digital card lookup returns no record, check the number and date against the issued card. For a paper card, follow the digitisation route. For a digital record that still cannot be found, contact the issuing exchange with the original card.

## Apply, track and save the issued card

1. **Open the Employment Exchange service on Sewa Setu.** Select the service that fits your card and read its Notice Board before choosing your verification route.
2. **Prepare the documents for your route.** The service page lists residence and age proof, education records and claim-specific certificates. Put marksheets and pass certificates in the order requested. Keep the originals for verification.
3. **Follow the verification instructions.** The Aadhaar-based service uses Aadhaar to verify demographics. The Notice Board limits the listed non-Aadhaar services to otherwise eligible applicants whose Aadhaar is on hold due to NRC issues and who are applying for Class III or IV recruitment. It directs those applicants to their Employment Exchange for manual document verification.
4. **Save the acknowledgement and the issued card.** Keep them separately. Where a recruitment asks for a valid registration card, check the number and renewal information on the issued document before entering it in the job form.

For example, the [Assam Police document checklist]({{base}}/assam-police-constable-document-verification/) shows where the Employment Exchange card appeared in the Constable advertisement. The [APSC registration guide]({{base}}/apsc-one-time-registration-assam/) explains how a recruitment application can ask for it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I register without Aadhaar?

Check the Sewa Setu Notice Board. When checked on 27 November 2026, it limited the listed non-Aadhaar services to eligible applicants with an NRC-related Aadhaar hold for Class III or IV recruitment. Those applicants are directed to their Employment Exchange for document verification.

### How do I renew an old paper Employment Exchange card?

The published manual sends paper-card holders to re-registration and the option for digitising or renewing a paper card. Digital-card holders can use the renewal lookup with the registration details.

### Is the application acknowledgement the Employment Exchange card?

The acknowledgement confirms submission. Use the issued registration certificate when a recruitment asks for a valid card.
