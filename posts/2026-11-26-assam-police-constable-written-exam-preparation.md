**Start with the real Constable Set A paper and use your errors to choose what to practise next.** The 5 December 2025 AB and UB advertisement specified an OMR written test with 100 multiple-choice questions worth 50 marks. Each correct answer earned half a mark. The test covered elementary arithmetic, English, reasoning, Assam topics, general knowledge and current affairs.

Work through the exact weak topic, such as [number system]({{base}}/questions/number-system/), [grammar]({{base}}/questions/grammar/) or [Assam geography]({{base}}/questions/assam-geography/). ## Frequently asked questions

### How many marks was the written test in the December 2025 advertisement?

The SLPRB notice specified 100 questions worth 50 marks in total, with half a mark for each correct answer and zero marks lost for a wrong answer.

### Where can I practise the Assam Police Constable 2025 paper?

The linked AxomRank Set A page has the questions and answers, with a link to the original booklet.

### What does “provisional key” mean on the practice page?

It identifies the published provisional answer source used for this archived booklet. Check the issuing board for a final key when you are reviewing an actual examination.
