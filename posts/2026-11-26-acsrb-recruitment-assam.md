**ACSRB stands for Assam College Services Recruitment Board.** Assam's 2023 amendment to the College Employees (Provincialisation) Act provides for the board to select and recommend candidates for teaching and non-teaching posts covered by the Act. As of 26 November 2026, I found the Gazette amendment and the Directorate of Higher Education's existing material, but could not verify a dated board-wide recruitment advertisement or application portal in those sources.

## Which document answers which question?

| What you want to know | Official source to open |
| --- | --- |
| What is ACSRB and what does the law assign to it? | The Assam Gazette amendment, especially the substituted Section 6. |
| What does the Directorate say about selecting Assistant Professors? | The Directorate of Higher Education's selection guideline for Assistant Professors and Librarians. Use the vacancy notice for the applicable form. |
| When can I apply, and for which posts? | The recruitment advertisement for that intake and any later corrigendum. Its document number and application instructions matter. |

The Gazette describes the board's statutory role. Keep a post-specific advertisement separately, since it will determine requirements such as the subject or post, the application channel and the documents to submit.

## What applicants can prepare now

For **teaching posts**, gather the degree and marks records relevant to your subject. Keep evidence of any NET, SET, SLET, PhD, work experience or other qualifications you would claim. Compare them with the eligibility language in the advertisement when it is available.

For **non-teaching posts**, organise education, identity and applicable category records. Keep your experience or skill certificates available if you intend to claim them. Check exact requirements in the individual post notice before submitting anything.

When you find an advert, record its number, issuer, post, deadline and official application link together. Return to the issuing department to check for corrigenda before you apply or pay a fee.

Teaching applicants can use the [Assam Assistant Professor application checklist]({{base}}/assam-assistant-professor-application-checklist/) and the [SLET North East vs UGC NET guide]({{base}}/slet-ne-vs-ugc-net/) to organise records before comparing them with the actual notice.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does ACSRB stand for?

The Assam Gazette uses Assam College Services Recruitment Board. The 2023 amendment provides for the board's selection and recommendation functions for covered college appointments.

### Is there an official ACSRB application date?

A board-wide date was unverified in the official sources checked on 26 November 2026. Look for the dated recruitment advertisement and any corrigenda from the issuing authority.

### Is ACSRB the same as AESRB?

ACSRB is the college recruitment board provided for in the College Employees Act amendment. AESRB is the Assam Engineering Service Recruitment Board and publishes its own recruitment notices.
