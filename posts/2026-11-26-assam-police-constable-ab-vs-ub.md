**AB means Armed Branch and UB means Unarmed Branch.** In the 5 December 2025 Constable advertisement, SLPRB specified HSLC or Class X for AB and Higher Secondary or Class XII for UB, from a government-recognised board or council. Applicants with Higher Secondary or above could apply for both and indicate their preferences.

## How the branch rules compare

| Item | Constable AB | Constable UB |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Educational qualification in Section 5.6 | HSLC or Class X | Higher Secondary or Class XII |
| Physical efficiency test marks | 60 for male candidates and 40 for female candidates | 40 |
| PET events | Race and long jump, plus chin-ups for male candidates | Race and long jump |
| Written test in Section 7.8 | The advertisement lists a common written-test format for AB and UB | The same format appears in the joint advertisement |

The qualification row is one part of eligibility. Read the rest of Section 5 and any corrigendum for the conditions that apply to your category and application.

## How to read the preference field

Start by checking the exact post names and the education requirement on the notice. If you are considering both branches, read the physical-test rules for each and note your preferred order before you complete the application. Save your acknowledgement so you can confirm what you selected later.

Use the [PST and PET guide]({{base}}/assam-police-constable-pst-pet/) to see the events and measurements in the December 2025 advertisement. The [recruitment guide]({{base}}/jobs/assam-police-constable-recruitment/) links to the official notice and later reopening notice.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a Class X candidate apply for UB under this advertisement?

The UB qualification in Section 5.6 was Higher Secondary or Class XII. The advertisement listed HSLC or Class X for AB, subject to the other eligibility conditions.

### Can a Class XII candidate choose both AB and UB?

The 5 December 2025 notice allowed applicants with Higher Secondary or above to apply for both and record their preference.

### Which branch includes a chin-up test?

The advertisement included it for male Armed Branch candidates. See the physical-test section of the notice for event and scoring rules.
