Start with two associations tested in ADRE: **Maniram Dewan and the 1857 rebellion**, and **Kanaklata Barua and the Quit India Movement at Gohpur**. The questions ask for both the event and the place, so learn each person with a short context clue.

## People, events and places

| Person | Association to remember |
| --- | --- |
| Maniram Dewan | The 1857 rebellion in Assam. The National Book Trust account describes his role in plans to challenge British rule. |
| Kanaklata Barua | The Quit India Movement. She led a flag procession at Gohpur in 1942. |
| Mukunda Kakati | The same 1942 Gohpur flag procession. ADRE pairs his name with Kanaklata's in a question about the place. |
| Kushal Konwar | Assam's Quit India history. A Government of India commemorative account records his execution at Jorhat Jail in 1943. |

## Frequently asked questions

### Which movement is Kanaklata Barua associated with?

She is associated with the Quit India Movement and the flag procession at Gohpur in 1942.

### Which uprising is Maniram Dewan associated with?

He is associated with the 1857 rebellion in Assam.

### Which place connects Kanaklata Barua and Mukunda Kakati?

Gohpur is the place tested by the linked ADRE question.
