**Keep your age and education records, photo and signature ready before you apply for an Assam government job.** Then use the vacancy notice to identify the residence, Employment Exchange, reservation and post-specific records that apply to you. Keep the uploaded files and the originals together so you can retrieve the exact documents you used.

## What to put in your application folder

| What the recruiter may check | What to prepare |
| --- | --- |
| Name and date of birth | The document your notice accepts as proof of age, such as an HSLC admit card or pass certificate where listed. Compare the spelling and date with the application. |
| Educational qualification | The marksheets and pass certificates needed to show the required level or subject. Check the achievement date against the notice. |
| Photograph and signature | Clear files, sized and formatted for the application portal you are using. |
| Assam residence or local registration | Check which [residence proof or PRC]({{base}}/assam-prc-for-government-jobs/) the notice accepts and whether it separately asks for an [Employment Exchange card]({{base}}/assam-employment-exchange-registration/). |
| Category or quota | Prepare the certificate for the claim you make, such as an [Assam caste certificate]({{base}}/assam-caste-certificate-online/), [OBC Non-Creamy Layer certificate]({{base}}/assam-obc-ncl-certificate/) or [EWS Income and Asset Certificate]({{base}}/assam-ews-certificate-state-vs-central-jobs/), if applicable. |
| Declarations and post-specific records | Check whether the notice asks for [Small Family Norms Form A]({{base}}/assam-small-family-norms-form-a/), a licence, work experience or any other evidence for the post or quota. |

## Turn the advertisement into a checklist for your vacancy

1. **Save the advertisement and any corrigendum.** Match the advertisement number, the exact post and the branch or recruitment category. Record the relevant eligibility date and the last date to submit the form.
2. **Mark the stage beside each document.** Use separate columns for online upload and in-person verification. Record the acceptable format, issuing authority and issue date where the notice specifies them.
3. **Review the submitted application.** Save the acknowledgement, application number, uploaded files and payment receipt where applicable. If you apply through [APSC One-Time Registration]({{base}}/apsc-one-time-registration-assam/), save the acknowledgement for the specific vacancy as well.
4. **Read the call letter before travelling.** Use it with the advertisement to confirm which originals and copies to bring, how to attest copies and which ID the centre accepts.

## Two examples from recruitment notices

**APSC Combined Competitive Examination, 2025.** In advertisement 01/2026, the commission listed an Employment Exchange registration certificate, PRC or caste certificate as proof of original inhabitant status. It separately required a valid Assam Employment Exchange registration card and prescribed Form A. If you use a PRC to prove residence, keep the Employment Exchange document for that separate requirement.

**Assam Police Constable AB and UB.** The December 2025 advertisement called for the uploaded originals and one set of self-attested photocopies at PET/PST document verification. Follow the [Constable document verification checklist]({{base}}/assam-police-constable-document-verification/) for the relevant sections of that advertisement.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do all Assam government jobs require a PRC and an Employment Exchange card?

Recruiters specify their own proof and registration requirements. Read the residence clause and the Employment Exchange clause separately for the vacancy you want.

### Should I keep both my marksheet and pass certificate?

Keep both for each qualification you claim. The Assam Police Constable advertisement, for example, lists a pass certificate and marksheet for the applicable qualification.

### Is the upload enough for document verification?

Follow the call letter and the recruitment notice. When originals and self-attested copies are specified, bring those along with any required ID, admit card or acknowledgement.
