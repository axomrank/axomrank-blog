The **Ahoms defeated the Mughal forces at Saraighat in 1671**, on the Brahmaputra near Guwahati. Lachit Borphukan led the Ahoms. Ram Singh of Amber led the Mughal expedition sent by Emperor Aurangzeb. Remember the two Mughal names separately because ADRE has asked for both.

## Saraighat at a glance

| What the question asks | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Year of the battle | 1671 |
| River | Brahmaputra |
| Ahom commander | Lachit Borphukan, also spelled Lachit Barphukan |
| Mughal commander in the field | Ram Singh of Amber |
| Mughal emperor | Aurangzeb |
| Ahom ruler at the time | Udayaditya Singha |

The Kamrup district account places Saraighat after two important events. The Ahoms recovered Guwahati in 1667, then fought Ram Singh's forces at Alaboi in 1669. The fighting at Saraighat was predominantly on the river. The Ahoms had smaller boats and won under Lachit's command.

## Frequently asked questions

### In which year was the Battle of Saraighat fought?

It was fought in 1671, on the Brahmaputra near Guwahati.

### Who led the Mughals at Saraighat?

Ram Singh of Amber commanded the Mughal forces. Emperor Aurangzeb appointed him to lead the expedition.

### Which Ahom king ruled during the Battle of Saraighat?

Udayaditya Singha was the Ahom ruler during the battle. Lachit Borphukan commanded the Ahom forces.
