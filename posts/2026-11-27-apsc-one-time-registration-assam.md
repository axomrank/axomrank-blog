**Create your One Time Registration (OTR) profile on the Assam Public Service Commission recruitment portal, then apply separately for each advertised post.** If you already have an APSC profile, start by logging into it. Use the advertisement to check the required uploads, fee and deadline before opening the post application.

## From account creation to a submitted application

| Stage | What to do | What to keep |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Register or sign in | New applicants use the registration link and an active mobile number and email. Existing users sign in with their registered details. | Access to the registered contact details |
| Complete the profile | Review identity, personal, address, category, education and applicable experience fields. Upload the files requested by the profile. | The correct supporting documents |
| Apply for the post | Open the Apply section in your account. Confirm the advertisement number and post before filling in the form. | The advertisement and application details |
| Preview and submit | Check the uploaded files, follow the fee or payment instructions that apply, and complete the final submission. | The submitted form and confirmation or payment record where applicable |

## What to prepare before you apply

The **CCE 2025 advertisement, No. 01/2026**, gives a concrete example. Its Appendix I called for legible PDF uploads, including date-of-birth evidence and the degree certificate with the marksheets in a single PDF. The same appendix separately listed a [valid Employment Exchange card]({{base}}/assam-employment-exchange-registration/), evidence of original inhabitancy and the [Small Family Norms declaration]({{base}}/assam-small-family-norms-form-a/). Check the file formats, sizes and documents in your own advertisement.

Open each selected upload in the preview before proceeding. Compare the name and date of birth with your profile, confirm that all required pages are legible, and make sure the advertisement number is still correct. The cited CCE notice said the form could not be edited after final submission.

## How to check if the application went through

Look for the submitted form and the confirmation described in your advertisement. For the cited CCE cycle, APSC instructed applicants to use the portal's **Check Status** option for a failed or pending payment and stated that a successful submission would generate an email or SMS. Use the contact details or technical support on the official portal if your status remains unclear.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I have to register a new APSC account for each post?

The portal provides a sign-in route for existing users. Sign in, then use the Apply section for each post you want to apply for.

### Does completing the APSC profile submit my application?

The completed profile lets you proceed to the advertised post. Submit that post's application separately and check its confirmation.

### Can I edit an APSC application after I submit it?

The cited CCE 2025 instructions required corrections before final submission. Follow the applicable advertisement and any correction notice for your own post.
