Coaching batches for ADRE in Assam often cost a few thousand rupees, and many good ones are worth it. But a lot of aspirants work, live far from Guwahati, or simply cannot pay. This plan is for them. It uses only free material. Every step is something you can do on a phone.

## What you need (all free)

- **The real papers.** All ten ADRE papers from 2022 and 2024, with answers, are in the open [AxomRank PYQ dataset](https://github.com/axomrank/PYQ). The 2024 answers follow SLRC's official final key.
- **Assam GK lessons.** The [AxomRank app]({{app}}/) has bilingual (English and Assamese) lessons on Assam history, Indian polity, geography, maths and reasoning, with spaced review. It works offline once loaded.
- **A notebook for mistakes.** This matters more than any book.

## Why the plan is weighted this way

In the 2022 and 2024 papers, [maths, English and reasoning were 57% of Grade III and 55% of Grade IV]({{base}}/adre-previous-year-questions-analysis/). Assam-specific GK was about one question in eight. So the plan gives skills daily time and treats Assam GK as a steady weekly block.

## The 8 weeks

| Week | Daily (about 1 hour) | Weekly block (about 3 hours) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Number system and arithmetic basics | Take one 2024 paper untimed. Mark every topic you could not answer. |
| 2 | Verbal reasoning sets | Assam history: the founding kings to the Mughal wars |
| 3 | English vocabulary and grammar | Assam geography: districts, rivers, borders and national parks |
| 4 | Geometry and mensuration | **Timed 2022 paper.** Review every mistake. |
| 5 | Logical and non-verbal reasoning | Assam culture: festivals, communities, music, literature and film |
| 6 | Mixed maths drills under time | Indian polity basics and Assam administration |
| 7 | English plus reasoning timed sets | **Timed 2024 paper.** Compare your score with week 4. |
| 8 | Revise the mistakes notebook only | Two full papers on separate days, in exam conditions |

Grade IV aspirants can swap half the English time for extra reasoning, because Grade IV papers carried far less English.

## Three habits that matter more than hours

1. **Review mistakes the next day and again a week later.** Spaced review is the most reliable way to remember facts. The AxomRank app schedules this for you.
2. **Time yourself from week 4.** Many aspirants know the answers but run out of time.
3. **Learn Assam GK from sources, not forwarded lists.** Popular GK lists often disagree, for example on [how many Ahom kings there were]({{base}}/ahom-kings-list/). Check facts against a named source.

## When coaching is worth paying for

Free self-study works best for people who can keep a routine. A paid batch can help if you need daily accountability, live doubt-clearing, or offline classes for the computer skill test. If you do pay, ask the institute for its past results and a free demo class, and keep using the real papers alongside.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I crack ADRE without coaching?

Many candidates prepare on their own. What matters most is regular practice on the real paper pattern, timed mocks, and reviewing mistakes. All of these can be done with free material.

### How many hours a day should I study for ADRE?

This plan assumes about one hour on weekdays plus a three-hour weekly block. If you have more time, add timed practice rather than more reading.
