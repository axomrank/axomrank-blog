**Check the exact recruitment notice to see which proof of residence it accepts and when to submit it.** Assam's public e-form is titled Permanent Residence Certificate for Higher Education. Recruitment notices use their own document lists. Read the residence-proof line together with the rest of the application checklist.

## What different Assam notices ask for

| Source | Instruction to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Assam's PRC e-form | The published service is for higher education. The form has its own residence and supporting-document fields. |
| APSC CCE 2025 advertisement 01/2026 | Appendix I lists a valid Employment Exchange registration card, PRC or caste certificate as proof of original inhabitancy. It also asks for a valid Employment Exchange card in a separate checklist item. |
| SLPRB Constable advertisement 727/2025/94 | Section 5.2 says selected applicants submit proof of residence after publication of the final select list. The advertisement separately required Employment Exchange registration. |

These examples show why it helps to check both the **document** and the **stage**. The same application can ask for a valid card in one field and another accepted form of evidence in a separate field.

## Read the notice before applying for a certificate

1. Find the exact advertisement and any corrigendum. Look for “original inhabitant”, “permanent resident” or “proof of residence” in the eligibility and document sections.
2. Write down the list of documents the notice accepts, how current they must be, and the deadline or verification stage.
3. Keep other required documents on their own list. If the notice separately asks for an Employment Exchange card, check the [Assam Employment Exchange registration and renewal guide]({{base}}/assam-employment-exchange-registration/).
4. If your intended document is unclear, use the recruiting body's notice or listed contact to confirm the acceptable proof before submitting.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is PRC compulsory for the APSC CCE 2025 application example?

Appendix I of advertisement 01/2026 gives alternatives for proof of original inhabitancy. It separately lists a valid Employment Exchange registration card.

### Which Assam PRC form is available online?

The Government of Assam's published e-form is labelled for higher education. Read your vacancy notice to see what it accepts for the job's residence requirement.

### Must I give residence proof at the same stage for every Assam job?

Follow the stages printed in your advertisement. The cited APSC example lists an application upload, while the cited SLPRB example specifies proof from selected candidates after the final list.
