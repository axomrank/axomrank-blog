**Practise making a document, a spreadsheet and a presentation for the ADRE Grade III computer skill test.** The SLRC notice for the July 2025 test assigned **25 marks** and specified different expected skills for Categories I, II and III. Use the table to read that archived syllabus and the exercise below to practise it.

## What the 2025 categories covered

| Category in the notice | Required qualification of the posts | Skills to practise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | HSSLC and/or HSSLC in Science | Basic document formatting, basic Excel formulas and presentation formatting and printing |
| II | Bachelor's degree | Tables in Word, intermediate Excel formulas and validation, and intermediate presentation formatting |
| III | Bachelor's degree or bachelor's degree in Library Science as applicable, with the listed computer certificate or diploma | References and lists in Word, lookups, pivot tables and charts in Excel, and tables and charts in a presentation |

The category followed the posts for which the candidate provisionally qualified. Check the official instructions that apply to your next test. The [HSSLC vs Graduate written-paper guide]({{base}}/adre-grade-3-hsslc-vs-graduate/) covers an earlier stage.

The files use fictional data. Open the CSV in a spreadsheet app and save your working copy in spreadsheet format before adding formulas.

1. **Word.** Format the note as a one-page office memo. Add a bold title, a small branch table and a short recommendation. For a Category III drill, insert a reference or footnote and use a numbered list for your proposed actions.
2. **Excel.** Total both numeric columns using formulas, then use a formula to find the largest number due back. For a Category II drill, add validation that prevents a negative number of books. For a Category III drill, create a branch-level pivot table and a chart from the source data.
3. **PowerPoint.** Make a slide that identifies the branch with the most books due back and shows its count. Add a clear title and source label. For a Category III drill, add a labelled chart and check print preview.
