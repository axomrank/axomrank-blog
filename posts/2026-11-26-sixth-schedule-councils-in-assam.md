Part I of the table under **paragraph 20 of the Sixth Schedule** names three areas in Assam: **North Cachar Hills District, Karbi Anglong District and Bodoland Territorial Areas District**. In an exam, you may see the constitutional area names or the names of their autonomous councils.

## Match the areas and councils

| Area named in the Sixth Schedule | Council name commonly used |
| --- | --- |
| North Cachar Hills District | North Cachar Hills Autonomous Council |
| Karbi Anglong District | Karbi Anglong Autonomous Council |
| Bodoland Territorial Areas District | Bodoland Territorial Council |

Use the constitutional table as your test for Sixth Schedule status. The term *autonomous council* also occurs in the names of bodies created under other legal arrangements.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which part of the Sixth Schedule lists Assam's autonomous districts?

The Assam entries are in Part I of the table under paragraph 20.

### Is Bodoland listed in the Sixth Schedule?

Yes. Part I lists the Bodoland Territorial Areas District. The associated council is the Bodoland Territorial Council.

### What should I memorise for a multiple-choice question?

Learn the three constitutional area names and match each to the corresponding council name. Exam questions may use either form.
