**Assam's online Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) form asks for a digital OBC or MOBC certificate number before fetching the caste record.** Open the state form, then gather the residence and income evidence it asks for. If you have a paper caste certificate, follow the [Assam digital caste certificate application guide]({{base}}/assam-caste-certificate-online/) first.

## Check the certificate the job actually asks for

The state caste certificate records an applicant's recognised OBC or MOBC community. The Assam NCL application starts from that caste record and separately asks for financial details. For a job, read the notice for the certificate name, required format, issuing authority and issue-date conditions. Keep the document used for that claim with the application receipt.

## Documents on the Assam NCL form

| What the form checks | What to gather |
| --- | --- |
| Digital caste record | The digital OBC or MOBC number and certificate file |
| Residence | One listed proof, which may include PRC, Aadhaar, EPIC, a land document, an electricity bill or an eligible ration card |
| Income evidence | The income certificate requested for the family circumstances shown in the form. The upload field gives examples of authorities for agriculturists and retired salaried parents. |
| Application form | The scan requested in the attachment section |
| Additional documents | Any applicable evidence specifically asked for in your case |

The published form marks which fields are mandatory and tells applicants to combine income certificates into one file if there is more than one. Open the form itself to check that the labels and requirements still agree with your documents.

## Prepare and submit the application

1. Open the [Assam NCL e-form](https://eforms.assam.gov.in/SPP/temp/Non%20Creamy%20Layer.jsp) or locate the service through Sewa Setu.
2. Use the digital caste certificate number to fetch the record. Compare the details with the certificate before proceeding.
3. Complete the address and financial-details sections, attach the documents, and review the form before submitting. Save the returned acknowledgement and the issued certificate for the recruitment.

For other kinds of proof, see the [PRC and Assam government job documents guide]({{base}}/assam-prc-for-government-jobs/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I apply for Assam NCL with only a paper OBC certificate?

The published e-form asks for a digital OBC or MOBC number and provides a caste certificate application link for applicants without one. Follow the instructions for your existing certificate or check with the issuing office.

### Is a PRC the only acceptable residence proof on the NCL form?

The form lists several options, including Aadhaar, EPIC, land documents and electricity bills. Check the conditions on the form for your chosen proof.

### Does one Assam NCL certificate format work for every vacancy?

Read the specific recruitment notice. Match the format, issuing authority and date conditions named for that vacancy.
