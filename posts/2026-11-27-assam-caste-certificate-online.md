**To apply for a caste certificate online in Assam, open the government caste certificate form, choose your notified category, and prepare proof of birth, residence and community.** The state procedure covers SC, ST(P), ST(H), OBC and MOBC and provides for digitally signed certificates. If you need the certificate for a job, match the category and format to that recruitment notice.

## Documents to prepare

| What you need | What the Assam guidelines list |
| --- | --- |
| Applicant's photograph | A recent passport-size photo. Check the upload size and file type on the form. |
| Date of birth | One accepted record, such as a birth certificate or an admit card from a recognised board. |
| Residence | An accepted record such as a PRC, voter ID or land document. Check whose record the form accepts. |
| Community | Your father's caste certificate or a recommendation from a caste, tribe or community organisation authorised by the state. |
| Existing paper caste certificate | Keep the original ready to scan if you are applying for a digitally signed certificate. |

The government e-form also displays fields for a local recommendation and legacy records. Check which fields are active after you select your category. If the form asks for evidence you do not have or its instructions are unclear, ask the issuing office which documents apply to your case before submitting.

## How to submit the Assam caste certificate application

1. Open the [Government of Assam caste certificate e-form](https://eforms.assam.gov.in/SPP/temp/CASTE%20CERTIFICATE.jsp?formId=EID359&locale=en_US&pdfPath=/SPP/temp/CASTE+CERTIFICATE.jsp). Dibrugarh district's government service directory also lists Mission Bhumiputra for these categories.
2. Select SC, ST(P), ST(H), OBC or MOBC as applicable. Enter the community name as it appears in the notified list, then review your name, date of birth and address against your records.
3. Attach the evidence requested for your application. Preview the form, submit it and save the acknowledgement. Use a mobile number you can access, since the form says status messages go to that number.
4. When the digitally signed certificate is issued, check your name and category and save the certificate number with the file. The state guidelines provide for digital certificates to be stored in DigiLocker.

If you need Non-Creamy Layer status for a vacancy, Assam's NCL form asks for the digital OBC or MOBC certificate number to retrieve your caste record. Once you have that number, use the [Assam Non-Creamy Layer application checklist]({{base}}/assam-obc-ncl-certificate/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I apply without my father's caste certificate?

Assam's standard procedure lists a recommendation from a state-authorised caste, tribe or community organisation as an alternative. Check the form and the issuing office's instructions for your category.

### Can I use an old paper caste certificate to apply for a digital one?

Yes. The state guidelines provide for holders of physical certificates to apply for a digitally signed certificate by uploading the original certificate along with the other documents requested.

### Does an Assam OBC caste certificate complete my Non-Creamy Layer application?

The digital OBC or MOBC number lets the Assam NCL form retrieve the caste record. The NCL form also asks for financial information and supporting records. Use the certificate format named in your vacancy notice.
