## Four questions worth revisiting

### Question 5. Convert the mass before setting up the ratio

Twelve sheets weigh 40 g. The problem asks how many sheets weigh 2⅔ kg. Convert 2⅔ kg to 8,000/3 g. Multiply that by 12 sheets per 40 g to get **800 sheets**. Keep the mass exact until the last step. [Question 5]({{base}}/question-papers/dme-assam-2025-grade-3-non-technical-question-paper/#q5)

### Question 6. Check both roots of the quadratic

For 2x² − 5x + 3 = 0, factor the left side as (2x − 3)(x − 1). Set each factor to zero. The roots are **3/2 and 1**. In a multiple-choice question, substitute each proposed root if the factorisation is slow to spot. [Question 6]({{base}}/question-papers/dme-assam-2025-grade-3-non-technical-question-paper/#q6)

### Question 8. Separate the shadow length from the whole base

After four seconds at 1.2 m/s, the girl is 4.8 m from the lamppost. Her height is 0.9 m and the lamp is 3.6 m above ground. If the shadow is *s* metres long, the larger triangle has a base of 4.8 + *s*. Similar triangles give 0.9/*s* = 3.6/(4.8 + *s*), so **the shadow measures 1.6 m**. [Question 8]({{base}}/question-papers/dme-assam-2025-grade-3-non-technical-question-paper/#q8)

### Question 42. Use the object pronoun after a preposition

The sentence asks for the missing pronoun after “for you and”. Read the shorter phrase “for me”. Adding “you and” keeps the object form, so the answer is **me**. [Question 42]({{base}}/question-papers/dme-assam-2025-grade-3-non-technical-question-paper/#q42)
