**Srimanta Sankardeva** is associated with Assam's Neo-Vaishnavite tradition and with **Ankiya Nat**, the one-act plays tested in ADRE. **Madhabdev** was a disciple who carried the tradition forward. Learn the people and the terms together so that you can recognise them in a matching question.

## Terms to keep separate

| Term | What to associate with it |
| --- | --- |
| Sattra | A Vaishnavite institution that also became a centre for learning and the arts |
| Ankiya Nat | The one-act plays associated with Sankardeva |
| Ankiya Geet | Songs composed for the plays |
| Madhabdev | A major disciple of Sankardeva who helped carry on the tradition |

Barpeta district's cultural account describes how sattras supported music, dance, drama and other arts. Its cultural forms page discusses the relationship between an Ankiya Nat and its songs.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the one-act play form associated with Sankardeva?

It is Ankiya Nat. The songs composed for the plays are called Ankiya Geet.

### Who was Madhabdev?

Madhabdev was a disciple of Sankardeva and an important figure in carrying forward the Vaishnavite tradition in Assam.

### What is a sattra?

A sattra is a Vaishnavite institution. Sattras have also served as centres for education, music, dance and drama.
