## Frequently asked questions

### Can I download APDCL and AEGCL Assistant Accounts Officer papers separately?

Yes. Each company and recruitment is labelled in its own section. Match both the company and paper part before using a booklet.

### What if two accounts papers have the same year?

Compare the company, paper number and subject printed on the cover. The same year can contain distinct recruitment papers.
