**PET measures performance in physical events. PST checks physical measurements.** For advertisement 727/2025/94, the State Level Police Recruitment Board (SLPRB) specified PET, then a medical examination and PST. PET carried marks. PST carried zero marks and required candidates to meet the prescribed standards.

## PET events in the December 2025 advertisement

| Candidate group | Race and qualifying time | Minimum long jump | Extra event |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Male candidates | 3,200 m in up to 14 minutes | 335 cm | Male AB candidates also needed at least four chin-ups |
| Female candidates | 1,600 m in up to eight minutes | 244 cm | Race and long jump as listed |

The race and jump were each worth up to 20 marks. The additional chin-up event was worth up to 20 marks for male AB candidates. The advertisement gives the scoring bands and the rules for valid attempts in Section 7.3.

## PST height standards in that advertisement

| Category named by SLPRB | Male height | Female height |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General, OBC, MOBC and SC | 162.5 cm | 154 cm |
| ST (P) and ST (H) | 160 cm | 152 cm |

See Section 5.7 for the male chest measurements, which use different category groupings from the height table. Use the original categories and any corrigendum that applies to your application.

## Before reporting

The advertisement asked candidates to bring their PET/PST admit card, proof of identity, original documents uploaded with the application and a set of self-attested copies. Compare that list with the instructions on your own admit card. The [recruitment guide]({{base}}/jobs/assam-police-constable-recruitment/) links to the SLPRB notice board for cycle-specific updates.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between PST and PET?

PST is the Physical Standard Test, which checks prescribed measurements. PET is the Physical Efficiency Test, which scores events such as the race and long jump.

### Did the December 2025 notice require chin-ups for all Constable candidates?

The chin-up event in Section 7.3 applied to male Constable AB candidates. The other groups had the events shown above.

### Does passing the physical minimum guarantee a written test call?

The advertisement uses a shortlist based on PET marks and category. Check the written-test shortlist for your recruitment.
